## Expenses
 
Create the expense report in Ledgerly and attach an itemised receipt for every line above $25. It must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Reports submitted later are rejected automatically and need director approval to reopen. Meal allowances are $75 per day for domestic travel and $95 per day for international travel.
